Chatyy — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

OneMundo ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Chatyy application and related services accessible through onemundo.com.br, superbora.com.br, and boraum.com.br. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our email, chat, video meeting, calendar, file management, and document collaboration services.

By using Chatyy, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and password — Used for IMAP authentication and account access.
  • Display name and profile photo — Visible to other users on the platform.

Contacts

  • Phone numbers and email addresses from your device contacts (with your permission) — Used solely to identify which of your contacts are registered on Chatyy so you can connect with them.

Communications

  • Email messages — Stored on our IMAP servers as part of standard email service.
  • Chat messages — Stored on our servers to provide message history and synchronization across your devices.
  • Status updates — Temporarily stored (24 hours) and visible to your contacts.

Files and Documents

  • Uploaded files, email attachments, and documents created within our editor — Stored on our servers to provide file management and collaboration features.

Device Permissions and Data

  • Camera and microphone — Accessed only during video and audio calls, with your permission.
  • Photo library — Accessed when you choose to send image attachments or set a profile photo.
  • Location — Accessed only when you explicitly choose to share your location in a chat message. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Push notification tokens — Collected to deliver notifications about new emails, chat messages, meeting invitations, and calendar events.

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address and basic connection metadata for security and abuse prevention.
  • Device type and platform (for delivering an optimized experience).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our email, chat, video meeting, calendar, file management, and document collaboration services.
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • Deliver push notifications for new messages, emails, and events.
  • Enable you to find and communicate with other registered users.
  • Facilitate real-time video and audio calls.
  • Store and synchronize your messages, files, and calendar events across devices.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • Improve and develop our services.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your email content, chat messages, or files for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on servers managed by OneMundo.
  • Email is stored using standard IMAP protocols with authenticated access.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text.
  • Authentication tokens are generated securely and expire after a period of inactivity.
  • File uploads are validated and filtered to prevent malicious content.
  • All connections to our servers are encrypted using TLS/SSL.
  • We implement file-level locking and access controls to protect data integrity.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Chatyy integrates with the following third-party services:

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications to your device. When you enable notifications, a device token is shared with Google's FCM service. Google's privacy policy applies to their handling of this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Expo Push Notification Service

We use Expo's push notification service as an intermediary for delivering notifications. Expo's privacy policy applies: https://expo.dev/privacy

WebRTC

Video and audio calls use WebRTC, an open standard for real-time communication. Call media (audio and video) is transmitted peer-to-peer when possible. TURN relay servers may be used when direct connections cannot be established. Call content is not recorded or stored by us unless you explicitly use a recording feature.

CDN Resources

We load certain interface components (such as the document editor and spreadsheet editor) from trusted content delivery networks (CDN). These CDN providers may collect standard web analytics data such as IP addresses.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — You can access your emails, chat messages, files, contacts, and calendar data at any time through the application.
  • Export — You can download your emails through any standard IMAP client. Chat messages, files, and documents can be exported through the application.
  • Correction — You can update your profile information, display name, and profile photo at any time.
  • Deletion — You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at suporte@chatyy.com.br. Upon request, we will delete your account, emails, chat messages, files, documents, push tokens, and any other personal data within 30 days.
  • Withdraw Consent — You can revoke device permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, location, notifications) at any time through your device settings.

6. Data Retention

  • Email messages are retained until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Chat messages are retained until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Status updates are automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Files and documents are retained until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Push notification tokens are automatically cleaned up after 30 days of inactivity.
  • Authentication tokens expire after 30 days of inactivity.

7. Children's Privacy

Chatyy is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at suporte@chatyy.com.br.

8. International Data

Our servers are located in the United States. If you access our services from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. By using our services, you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you through the application or via email.
